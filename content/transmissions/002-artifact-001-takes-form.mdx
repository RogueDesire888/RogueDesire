Every chapter leaves something behind.

For Ascension, it is an idol. A figure with its arms raised, held inside an obsidian tablet. Chrome lines carved into dark metal. Violet light caught in the cracks.

The front carries the idol. The back carries the name.

The night disappears. The object remembers.

This is a concept design. Production details will be confirmed before anything is final.
